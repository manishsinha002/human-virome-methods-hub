# Governance

This repository is an initial community prototype. It is not an official Human Virome Program standard unless formally endorsed.

## Proposed roles
- Maintainers: repository administration and releases
- Scientific editors: evidence review by domain
- Contributors: additions, corrections, and benchmarks

## Review policy
Material recommendations require at least one scientific-editor review. Claims of superior performance should preferentially cite independent benchmarks. Releases associated with the manuscript should be tagged and archived.
