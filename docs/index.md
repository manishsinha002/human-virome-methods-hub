## A methods resource, not just a tool list

Tool names alone do not tell you whether a method fits a specimen, recovers the viral fraction you care about, or supports the biological claim you want to make. This hub puts those decisions and their evidence side by side.

## Keep claims proportional to evidence

<div class="evidence-pathway" aria-label="Evidence pathway">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Sequence detection</strong><small>Is there credible viral signal?</small></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Genome reconstruction</strong><small>How complete and well supported is it?</small></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Persistence or activity</strong><small>What do time and RNA evidence show?</small></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Host linkage</strong><small>Is the association supported independently?</small></div>
  <div><span>05</span><strong>Biological function</strong><small>Which predictions need validation?</small></div>
</div>

Each step supports a different level of inference. Read the [evidence standards](evidence-standards.md) alongside the methods matrix, and distinguish candidate detection from confirmation, prediction from experiment, and association from causation.
